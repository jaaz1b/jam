Settings

Sources, your Discord status, and app options.

Sources

Any music website that plays without DRM (like Monochrome). Works right away, no reinstall.
Open with The service Jam opens when it starts. Switch any time with the buttons at the top.
Start where I left off Open the last service you used instead.
Keep switched-away sources ready Switching back within this time is instant. Shorter uses less memory and battery. The service you switch away from is paused.

Discord

Rich Presence Show the currently playing song on your Discord profile.
Account …
Show when paused Keep the card up while the track is paused. Discord has no paused state, so the progress bar is dropped rather than left running.
Hide details Say only that music is playing. No title, artist, album, artwork, progress bar or buttons leave your phone.

Discord setup

  

    
Discord Application ID
      

        
        
      

      

        Use the same application as Jam on your computer. One-time setup in the Discord Developer Portal:
        
    
          
  1. Open your Jam application (or click New Application and name it Jam). Copy the Application ID into the box above.
    2. 
          
  2. OAuth2 → Redirects: add jamdiscord://oauth2/callback (tap to copy) and save.
    3. 
          
  3. OAuth2: turn on Public Client and save.
    4. 
          
  4. Discord Social SDK → Getting Started: fill in the short form to enable the Social SDK. If it asks, create a developer team and move the app into it.
    5. 
          
  5. Come back here and tap Sign in with Discord.
