Settings

Sources, your Discord status, and app options.

Sources

Any music website that plays without DRM (like Monochrome). Works right away, no reinstall.
Open with The service Jam opens when it starts. Switch any time from the title bar or with Ctrl+1–9.
Start where I left off Open the last service you used instead.
Keep switched-away sources ready Switching back within this time is instant. Shorter uses less memory. The service you switch away from is paused.

Discord

Rich Presence Show the currently playing song on your Discord profile.
Show when paused Keep the card up while the track is paused. Discord has no paused state, so the progress bar is dropped rather than left running.
Hide details Say only that music is playing. No title, artist, album, artwork, progress bar or buttons leave your machine.

Status

Status text What Discord shows next to your name in the member list. The profile card always shows the app name.
App name Replaces “Jam” wherever {app} appears: lines, button labels, hover text. Use {source} for the service you're listening on (e.g. “Spotify”). The “Listening to …” header itself is your Discord application's name — rename it in the Developer Portal.
Paused label Added in front of line 2 while paused, e.g. “Paused · Neon Coast”. Leave empty to add nothing.

Connection

Discord Application ID …
  1. Open the Discord Developer Portal and click New Application.
  2. Name it Jam — that's the “Listening to …” name friends see.
  3. Copy the Application ID from General Information and paste it above.
  4. Optional: under Rich Presence → Art Assets, upload discord-asset-jam.png with the key jam.

App

Keep playing in the tray Closing the window hides it to the system tray instead of quitting.
Reduce animations Makes the site's animations and transitions instant. Saves CPU and GPU, especially on laptops. Applies right away.
