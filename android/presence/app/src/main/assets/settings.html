Jam Presence

What you play in Apple Music, on your Discord profile.

Setup

1See what's playing Turn on Jam Presence in Android's notification access. That's how Android lets an app see what Apple Music is playing (the song on your lock screen). Your notifications aren't read.
Says “Restricted setting”? Open App info, tap ⋮ (top right) ▸ Allow restricted settings, then tap Allow here again.
2Sign in with Discord A Discord application of yours, then one tap to sign in. Steps in Discord setup below.
3Allow notifications For the quiet “Showing on Discord” notification that keeps the connection alive.
4No battery limits So Android doesn't cut the Discord connection while your screen is off.
✓ All set. Play something in Apple Music and it shows on Discord.

Players

The songs of the players switched on here go to Discord. When several play, the one that started last is shown. A player shows up in this list once it has played something.

Show when paused Keep the card up while the track is paused. Discord has no paused state, so the progress bar is dropped rather than left running.
Hide details Say only that music is playing. No title, artist, album, artwork, progress bar or buttons leave your phone.
Stay connected Keeps a quiet notification up so Android doesn't close the Discord connection in the background. Without it, your status can stop updating while the screen is off.

Find covers & links
        Players give Android their cover as a picture, which Discord can't show. This looks the song up in Apple's public catalog for a cover Discord can show and the song's Apple Music link (the button). Only the title, artist and album are sent.

      
Fallback image
        
        An https:// image URL (Jam's jar by default), or an Art Asset key from your Discord application.

Discord setup

  

    
Discord Application ID
      

        
        
      

      

        One-time setup in the Discord Developer Portal:
        
    
          
  1. Click New Application. Its name is what Discord shows after “Listening to”, so call it Apple Music (or Jam, or anything). Copy the Application ID into the box above and tap Save.
    2. 
          
  2. OAuth2 → Redirects: add jampresence://oauth2/callback (tap to copy) and save.
    3. 
          
  3. OAuth2: turn on Public Client and save.
    4. 
          
  4. Discord Social SDK → Getting Started: fill in the short form to enable the Social SDK. If it asks, create a developer team and move the app into it.
    5. 
          
  5. Come back here and tap Sign in with Discord.
    6. 
        

        Already have a Jam application? You can use it instead: just add the redirect above to it (keep Jam's own).
